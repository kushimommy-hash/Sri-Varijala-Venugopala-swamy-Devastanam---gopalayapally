About the Temple

Sri Varijala Venugopala Swamy Devasthanam is a sacred Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Krishna in the divine form of Sri Venugopala Swamy. Devotees from nearby villages visit the temple to seek blessings, peace, prosperity and spiritual guidance. The temple celebrates important Vaishnava festivals with great devotion and offers daily worship to the Lord.
