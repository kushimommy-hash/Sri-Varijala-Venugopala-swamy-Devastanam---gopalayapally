About the Temple

Sri Varijala Venugopalaswamy Devasthanam is a sacred temple dedicated to Lord Sri Venugopalaswamy, a divine form of Lord Krishna. Devotees visit the temple seeking peace, prosperity, and divine blessings.

Temple Services

  • Daily Archana
  • Abhishekam
  • Special Poojas
  • Festival Celebrations
  • Annadanam
